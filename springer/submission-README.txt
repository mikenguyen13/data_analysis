MANUSCRIPT PACKAGE


CONTENTS

data_analysis_practice.tex
    The manuscript. Eleven chapters, written in R Markdown and produced with
    bookdown. It uses the default LaTeX book class rather than the Springer
    monograph template, on the understanding that production reformats during
    XML conversion. It can be supplied in your template instead on request.

    The package arrives as three archives, because of email attachment limits:
    702652-1 holds this file, the manuscript and the bibliographies, and
    702652-2 and 702652-3 hold the figures. Unpack all three into the same
    folder and the relative paths in the .tex resolve.

    A typeset PDF of the manuscript, 515 pages, is available on request. It is
    too large to attach here and is for reference rather than production.

references-springer.bib
    The consolidated bibliography the .tex uses. 202 entries, exactly those the
    volume cites, pruned from the 1,825 entries of the larger work.

bib-by-chapter/
    One bibliography per chapter, holding exactly the references that chapter
    cites, for citation linking. 211 entries across the eleven chapters, 202 of
    them unique, the difference being sources that two chapters both cite and
    which therefore belong in both lists. Chapters 6 and 10 cite nothing, so
    their files are empty.

*_files/figure-latex/
    The figures, 94 of them, at the relative paths the .tex refers to. Vector
    PDF throughout, with one exception: the decision tree in chapter 11 is a
    raster diagram. Every figure is generated by code printed in the manuscript,
    so any of them can be regenerated in another format or at another
    resolution.

NOTES

Abstracts and keywords. Every chapter opens with an abstract of at most 200
words and six keywords, inside a block immediately after the chapter title.

Grayscale. The figures distinguish their series by line type, point shape,
hatching or luminance rather than by hue, so they read correctly when printed in
grayscale. Three cannot be treated that way and need to print in colour:

    Chapter 5   report-amatheme-npg
                Demonstrates a journal colour palette, so grayscale defeats its
                purpose.

    Chapter 7   diamonds-specr-specification-curve
                robustness-rdf-spec-curve
                Specification curves drawn by package plotting functions that
                colour estimates by sign internally, where colour is the
                encoding rather than decoration.

References. The .tex carries a single bibliography. The per-chapter mapping is
supplied separately in bib-by-chapter/ rather than restructured into the LaTeX,
because doing the latter would change the citation machinery across the whole
manuscript to produce something the XML conversion derives anyway.

Third-party material. There is none. Every figure is generated by code in the
manuscript, every dataset ships in an open-source R package or is simulated by
the code itself, and there are no reproduced figures, photographs, screenshots
or quoted extracts requiring permission.
